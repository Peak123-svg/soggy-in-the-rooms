SOGGY CAT IN THE ROOMS

Help sog cat navigate through rooms. Grey lockers hide from standard entities, Blue lockers hide from the Searcher!

ENTITY GUIDEBOOK

Angry Sog

Type: Rusher
A fast entity that charges down the hallway. Hide in either Grey or Blue lockers to survive.

Evil Sog

Type: Rebounder
An entity playing a music box that bounces back and forth across the rooms multiple times. Hide until it fully passes!

Soggy Searcher

Type: Locker Checker
A sneaky new entity that explicitly checks Grey Lockers! If it finds you in a grey locker, you will be caught. Use Blue Lockers to hide from this entity safely.
